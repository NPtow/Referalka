# HR-Tech Taxonomy

## A. Talent Acquisition Core

- sourcing
- ATS
- recruiting CRM
- recruiting automation
- programmatic job distribution
- career site infrastructure
- candidate screening
- assessment / testing
- interview scheduling
- video interviewing
- background checks / references
- offer / hiring workflow

## B. Referral / Network Hiring

- employee referrals
- external referral marketplace
- warm-intro recruiting
- expert network hiring

## C. Staffing / Marketplace

- freelance talent marketplace
- direct hire marketplace
- agency marketplace
- talent cloud
- internal talent marketplace

## D. AI-Native Hiring

- AI recruiter
- AI sourcing
- AI screening
- AI interviewer
- AI talent intelligence
- AI candidate copilot
- AI recruiter copilot

## E. Candidate-Side Career Products

- job search copilot
- resume / application tooling
- career coaching
- interview prep
- salary transparency
- referral access for candidates

## F. Employer Brand / Top-of-Funnel

- employer branding
- job marketing
- talent community
- campus / early career hiring

## G. HR Core Adjacent

- onboarding
- HRIS
- payroll
- L&D
- performance
- engagement
- internal mobility
- workforce planning

## Правила разметки

- Каждой компании назначать `primary_segment`.
- Если продукт реально лежит на стыке, добавлять `secondary_segment`.
- Не путать сегмент продукта с business model.
- Не путать сегмент продукта с ICP.
